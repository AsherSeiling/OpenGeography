Ribbon icons — sample for approval

Twelve icons at 32 px (large buttons) and 16 px (small buttons), light and dark, beside the one-colour icon each command has today. A dark outline and two or three fills from one fixed palette; the 16 px drawings drop fine detail and use a heavier line. Our own drawings; ArcGIS Pro was a style reference only.

Every shape has the dark outline, so it is readable whatever its fill; the amber measure ruler and zoom handle rely on it. The two dashed selection rectangles have no solid outline, so they use the darker amber-d and grey-d tones instead (checked: 4:1 or better).
